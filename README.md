# Thailand Digital Arrival Card (TDAC) Guide

A comprehensive, step-by-step guide for international travelers to complete their digital arrival card (TDAC) before traveling to Thailand using the official mobile application.

---

## 📌 Pre-Departure Checklist
Ensure you have the following documents and details ready before starting the application process:
* **Valid Passport**: Bring the exact physical passport you will present upon arrival.
* **Travel Itinerary**: Arrival and departure dates, flight numbers, and travel method.
* **Accommodation Details**: Name and full address of your accommodation in Thailand.
* **Contact Information**: Active telephone number and email address.
* **Health Information**: Must be completed within 72 hours before arrival.

---

## 📱 Required Application
Download the official integrated platform by Thai Immigration before your travel:

### THIM - Thai Immigration
Used to create your digital arrival card, submit travel info, and manage health declarations.
* 🌐 **Google Play Store**: [Download THIM for Android](https://google.com)
* 🍏 **Apple App Store**: [Download THIM for iOS](https://apple.com)

---

## 🗺️ Step-by-Step Application Process

### 1. Account Setup & Trip Initialization
* Open the **THIM** app and create a new account (or sign in using an OTP/password).
* Tap **Get Started** or select **Arrival Card** from the main menu.
* Tap **Start a New Trip** and read through the entry instructions.
* Name your trip, select your boarding country (e.g., Malaysia), and your purpose of travel.

### 2. Arrival & Departure Details
* Input your **Arrival Date**, mode of travel (e.g., Air), and your specific flight number.
* Input your **Departure Date** and returning flight information.
* Provide the hotel name, full address, province, and postal code of your accommodation in Thailand.
* Review your trip itinerary summary carefully before proceeding.

### 3. Traveler Profile & Passport Verification
* In the traveler section, tap **Scan Passport** or choose *Select from Profile* to autofill details.
* Verify that all captured passport information (name, gender, date of birth) matches your document perfectly.
* Complete your remaining profile info by entering your occupation, active phone number, and email address.
* Review the final traveler summary and tap **Done**.

### 4. Health Declaration (Crucial Timing)
* Select **Update Traveler** to fill out the mandatory **Health Declaration**.
* ⚠️ **Important**: Do not complete this step earlier than **72 hours** before your scheduled arrival.
* Once submitted accurately, your unique **Trip QR Code** will be generated.

---

## ✈️ Before Going to the Airport
* [ ] Verify that your **Trip QR Code** is saved or screenshotted on your device.
* [ ] Double-check that your health declaration status is completed and active.
* [ ] Ensure all submitted data directly matches your **physical passport**.
